      How the numbers are calculated
      • Every number reads from the published Bridgeland Science Olympiad Build Tracker Google Sheet. Google refreshes published data about every 5 minutes, so edits show up here shortly after, or press Refresh data.
      • Best team: for each event at each invitational, only the best Bridgeland placement counts, since one team goes to state.
      • A medal means placing within that invitational's medal cutoff (set on the sheet's Lists tab).
      • Ready: in-person medal rate at least — and average in-person place — or better. Close: at least — and — or better. Otherwise Not yet.
      • Satellite results (online invitationals) are shown but do not count toward readiness.
      • Captain's call: when a captain picks Ready, Close or Not yet in the State ready? (captain) column of a Summary tab, that choice is used everywhere on this page in place of the calculated readiness, and is marked "captain".
